Re-encode site photos

The homepage photos ship as PNGs up to 3.6 MB, each with a chain of Webflow-generated srcset variants beside it — 25 MB of images for a page that never shows any of them larger than about 700 px.

This encodes one right-sized WebP per photo, using the largest source available and a target width matched to how big it actually renders. One good WebP replaces the whole variant chain. Originals are left alone; the PNGs stay as masters and are dropped at build time.
